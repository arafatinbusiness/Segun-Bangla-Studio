WORKFLOW:

  1. Admin in Main Portal
     ↓
  2. Clicks "Create Reel" button
     ↓
  3. Opens: studio.segunbangla.com/studio?article=ABC123
     ↓
  4. Studio fetches article from Firestore
     ↓
  5. Admin sees 3-pane editor:
     • Left: Images & music
     • Center: Live 9:16 preview
     • Right: Settings
     ↓
  6. Admin customizes reel:
     • Adds/reorders images
     • Selects music
     • Chooses template
     • Sets duration
     ↓
  7. Admin clicks "Render"
     ↓
  8. Server generates MP4 (30-90 seconds)
     ↓
  9. Admin downloads MP4
     ↓
  10. Admin uploads to:
      • Facebook Reels
      • Instagram Reels
      • TikTok
      • YouTube Shorts
